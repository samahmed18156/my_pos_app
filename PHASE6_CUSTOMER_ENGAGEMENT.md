# BKPOS Phase 6 — Customer Engagement & Promotions

Phase 6 builds on Phase 5 without removing existing POS, accounting, inventory, security, BI, operational-control, or resilience features.

## Included
- Customer segmentation by completed, non-voided sales.
- Spend-based VIP/Loyal/Active/Occasional/Inactive segments.
- Estimated loyalty points for management analytics (1 point per R10 of qualifying spend).
- Promotion catalogue with percentage, fixed-amount, and Buy X Get Y definitions.
- Promotion date/minimum-spend validation.
- CSV export of customer segments.
- Utility-menu integration with existing permission gate.

Promotion definitions are management data only in this phase; existing checkout pricing is deliberately unchanged so current sales behavior remains regression-safe.
