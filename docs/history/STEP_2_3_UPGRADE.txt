STEP 2 + STEP 3 COMPLETED

STEP 2 — QUOTATION TO INVOICE
- F9 Quotation remains a separate saved quotation workflow.
- Converting a quotation carries customer ID, customer type, customer number and all quotation lines into a new open invoice.
- Converted quotations are protected against duplicate conversion.
- A saved quotation cannot be converted twice.
- Stock is unchanged by quotation save/conversion; stock changes only at invoice checkout.

STEP 3 — CUSTOMER ACCOUNTS / DEBTORS
- Customer & Pricing remains the single customer master.
- Customer Accounts / Balances provides the debtor ledger and customer statements.
- Customer payments are recorded against the selected customer account.
- Customer Aging provides 0–30, 31–60, 61–90, 91–120 and 120+ day buckets.
- F12 Payment now includes CREDIT ACCOUNT.
- Credit Account requires a selected customer and an active credit limit.
- Credit sales are posted to the customer ledger in the same database transaction as the sale.
- Credit-account returns reduce the debtor balance and restore physical stock.
- sales_history now stores customer linkage for account/reporting purposes.
- No separate legacy Debtors Maintenance workflow is exposed in the final menu.

IMPORTANT BUSINESS RULE
- Retail / Bulk / Wholesale pricing remains unchanged.
- Selling prices remain VAT-inclusive and VAT is calculated at 15/115.
