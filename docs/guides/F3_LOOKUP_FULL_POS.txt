F3 LOOKUP STANDARD

Main Invoice:
- F3 on Barcode/Code or the Product Lookup button = Product Lookup.
- F3 on Customer Account/Customer Name or Customer Lookup button = Customer Lookup.

Quotation:
- F3 on customer fields = Customer Lookup.
- F3 on product field = Product Lookup.

GRN:
- F3 on supplier fields = Supplier Lookup.
- F3 on product field = Product Lookup.

Customer selection stores customer_id/customer_type on the current invoice so customer pricing can use the same selected customer.
