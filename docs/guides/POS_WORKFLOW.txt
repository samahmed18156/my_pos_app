POS WORKFLOW — SINGLE SOURCE OF TRUTH

PRODUCTS
- Product Management is the only menu option for creating and maintaining products.
- New Product starts with SOH = 0.
- Category and Supplier are controlled dropdowns.
- Existing product editing and wholesale/bulk pricing are opened from Product Management.
- No stock, minimum stock, reorder quantity or unit/packing fields are entered in Product Management.

STOCK
- GRN is the only workflow that receives supplier stock into SOH.
- Sales reduce SOH.
- Returns/Credit Notes restore SOH.
- Product lookup is read-only and cannot create products.

DUPLICATE WORKFLOWS REMOVED
- Legacy StockWindow no longer provides Add New Product or stock editing.
- Product Pricing & Wholesale is no longer a separate top-level menu item.
- Product Master/Pricing is an internal detail screen opened from Product Management only.
