PRODUCT MASTER — STOCK CONTROL UPDATE

Product Master is now for product identity, categories, suppliers, pricing and wholesale/bulk pricing.

Removed from Product Master UI:
- Minimum Stock
- Reorder Qty
- Unit of Sale
- Packing / Case Qty
- Stock on Hand (SOH) display/edit area

Important:
- Existing database columns are retained for compatibility with other POS modules.
- Editing an existing product in Product Master does NOT change SOH.
- Creating a new product starts SOH at 0.
- SOH is updated by GRN, sales, returns/credit notes and stock adjustment tools.
- GRN increases SOH by the quantity received.

This prevents accidental manual stock changes from Product Master.
