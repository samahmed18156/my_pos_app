GRN Supplier Credit Note workflow

A CREDIT NOTE button is now available directly on the GRN screen.

Workflow:
1. Open GRN / Goods Received.
2. Select an existing supplier with F3.
3. Click CREDIT NOTE.
4. The supplier is automatically carried into the Supplier Credit Note screen.
5. Only stock previously received from that supplier is listed.
6. Select a stock line and enter the quantity being returned.
7. Choose the reason: Damaged Stock, General Return, Expired Stock, Incorrect Delivery, or Other.
8. Credit value is automatically calculated as returned quantity x supplier cost.
9. Posting the credit note reduces product SOH by the returned quantity.
10. The calculated value is posted as a supplier credit and reduces the supplier outstanding balance.
11. The return is linked to the original GRN item so the same received quantity cannot be credited twice.

Example:
10 units received at R100 each.
Return 2 damaged units.
SOH: -2 units.
Supplier credit: R200.
Supplier outstanding: -R200.
