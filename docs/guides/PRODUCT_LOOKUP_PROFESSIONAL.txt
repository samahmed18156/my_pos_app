PRODUCT LOOKUP WORKFLOW

F3 Product Lookup is a sales-screen product selector only.
- Search by description or barcode.
- Select a product with Enter or double-click.
- No product creation, editing, pricing, stock receiving, or stock adjustment is available here.
- Retail price and SOH are display information; stock is changed by sales, GRN, and returns.

Price Lookup is view-only for product, category, supplier, cost, retail price, SOH, and active status.
