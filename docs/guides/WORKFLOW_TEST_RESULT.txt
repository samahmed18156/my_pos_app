POS WORKFLOW TEST / PRODUCT-STOCK BOUNDARY

PASS
- Product Management creates products.
- New products start with SOH = 0.
- Product Details & Pricing only maintains existing product details, retail price, cost and Retail/Bulk/Wholesale pricing.
- Product Details & Pricing never edits SOH, minimum stock, reorder quantity, unit-of-sale or packing controls.
- GRN selects existing products using F3 and receives stock into SOH.
- Sales reduce SOH.
- Returns/Credit Notes restore SOH.
- Product Lookup is read-only.

TESTED STOCK FLOW
Product created -> SOH 0 -> GRN +5 -> SOH 5 -> Sale -2 -> SOH 3 -> Return +1 -> SOH 4.

VAT
Selling prices are VAT-inclusive. VAT = gross * 15 / 115.

MENU
Product Management is the only product creation/maintenance entry. Product Details & Pricing is opened from Product Management; it is not a separate top-level workflow.
