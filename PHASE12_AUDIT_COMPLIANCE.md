# BKPOS Phase 12 — Audit & Compliance Center

Phase 12 adds a read-only management review screen that consolidates existing audit, cash, sales, return, stock and branch-sync exception signals.

## Included
- Compliance score and risk counts
- Audit activity and failed-login monitoring
- Open-shift and cash-variance findings
- Voided sales and return activity
- Negative-stock detection
- Staged branch-sync conflict detection
- CSV export
- Utility menu integration

## Safety
This phase is additive and read-only. It does not post transactions, alter stock, change prices, modify accounts, or delete/replace existing features.
