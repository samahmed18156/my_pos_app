# Phase 3 — Operational Control & Automation

Phase 3 builds on the Phase 2 Business Intelligence dashboard without removing existing functionality.

## Included
- Operational Control Center under Utility.
- Category performance rollups.
- Fast/slow product velocity using units sold per day.
- Stock valuation snapshot.
- Reorder recommendations using minimum stock plus lead/safety demand.
- Sales target versus actual performance cards.
- CSV export of the management control tables.
- Voided/incomplete sales excluded from operational sales analytics.

The control center is read-only: it does not post sales, stock, accounting, pricing, returns, or master-data changes.
