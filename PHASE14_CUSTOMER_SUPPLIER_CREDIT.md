# BKPOS Phase 14 — Customer & Supplier Credit Management

Phase 14 adds a read-only management center for debtor and creditor exposure.

## Customer credit
- Current account balance and credit-limit utilization
- Available credit
- Open invoices and overdue (>30 day) exposure
- Risk classification: OK, WATCH, HIGH, OVER LIMIT, or NO LIMIT
- Customer ageing detail

## Supplier credit
- Purchases, payments, supplier credits and outstanding balance
- Credit-limit utilization and payment terms
- Risk classification
- Supplier ageing detail where GRN outstanding balances are available

## Controls
- Read-only analytics; no financial posting is performed.
- Existing customer, supplier, payment, invoice and GRN records are preserved.
- CSV export is provided for management review.
